# Chapter 1: The Docking Permit

The airlock hissed open and Captain Vex stepped onto the dock, scanning the crowd.

**Captain Vex:** You're late.

**Mira:** The station's been scrambling your docking permit for an hour.

Mira looked up from her console and grinned.

**Tobb:** Permits are the least of your worries, Captain. Someone has been asking about your cargo.

**Captain Vex:** Who?

**Tobb (quietly):** Nobody I'd trust.

A soft chime sounded across the dock.

**Station AI:** Warning. Unregistered vessel approaching dock four.

# Chapter 2: Contact

Vex turned toward the viewport and smiled. Whatever was coming, she had been waiting for it.

**Captain Vex (shouting):** Mira, open a channel!

**Mira (nervously):** Channel open. They're not answering.
